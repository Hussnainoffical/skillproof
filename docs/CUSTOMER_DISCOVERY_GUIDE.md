# Customer discovery guide

Use this before adding real applicant documents or building buyer-specific integrations. Explain that SkillProof is a prototype, not an official service. Ask about work, not personal case details; do not collect names, documents, or confidential employer information.

## Interview outline (25–30 minutes)

1. **Context:** What role do you play in qualification recognition or international hiring? Which occupation, authority, region, and source-country cases do you handle most?
2. **Recent case:** Walk through the most recent case from receiving documents to sending it for a decision. What happened step by step? Which parts took the longest?
3. **Evidence work:** How do you know which items are required? Where is that checklist from, who updates it, and how do you record its version/date?
4. **Rework:** What was missing, unclear, inconsistent, translated, or hard to verify? How often do you ask for more documents? What does “verification” mean in your process?
5. **Current tools:** Which authority portals, adviser networks, spreadsheets, document systems, and commercial platforms do you use? What works well? What remains manual?
6. **Impact:** How many cases per month? Typical preparation/review time? Which errors create repeat work or delay? How is the cost currently measured?
7. **Prototype reaction:** Show the synthetic case only. Which part would you trust, reject, or need to change? Would source page links or a checklist packet fit your process?
8. **Buying path:** Who would approve a pilot and budget? What security, data residency, procurement, or legal checks would be required? What result would justify a paid trial?
9. **Next step:** Would you evaluate a synthetic or explicitly permissioned/redacted case with agreed measures? Who else should be interviewed?

## Avoid leading questions

Do not ask “Would you use this?” or “Is this revolutionary?” Ask for recent behavior, actual workarounds, existing spend, unresolved cases, and evidence of a committed next step. Do not tell participants that SkillProof can authenticate documents or decide equivalence.

## Decision log template

For each interview, record only role/organization type, date, case volume range, workflow steps, time range, rework causes, current tools, stated buyer, constraints, quote with permission, and next action. Keep identifiable notes out of this repo unless the participant explicitly authorizes storage and the approved retention/security process exists.

| Signal | Continue threshold | Evidence to collect |
|---|---:|---|
| Independent organizations with recurring manual evidence review | 3 or more | Walkthrough of recent workflow and recurring work step |
| Named owner and measurable outcome | Required for each pilot candidate | Role, success measure, baseline, and decision path |
| Permissioned evaluation commitment | At least 1 organization before real pilot build | Written scope and permitted synthetic/redacted sample plan |
| Existing product already solves the workflow | Any strong confirmation triggers reconsideration | Side-by-side feature/workflow comparison with user evidence |

## Do not proceed to real data without

Qualified reviewer oversight, documented lawful data process, retention/deletion schedule, security/privacy review, contracts where needed, risk classification and legal advice, and a signed pilot scope. This document is a research guide, not a substitute for those approvals.
