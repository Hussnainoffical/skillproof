# Market evidence and competitor boundary

**Research snapshot:** 2026-10-28 · **Conclusion:** problem signals exist; product demand and willingness to pay remain unproven.

## Need signals

- Germany's 2025 foreign-qualification recognition statistics report about 76,200 new applications, up roughly 7% on 2024 and nearly twice the level five years earlier. This is workflow volume, not the number of potential buyers for SkillProof. [BIBB / Recognition in Germany](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/pro/news-evaluation-recognition-statistics-2025.php)
- The European Commission's 2026 impact assessment describes fragmented recognition procedures, incomplete applications, and employer difficulty verifying cross-border credentials. In one SME panel, 25 of 63 respondents who had hired abroad reported verification difficulty. The panel is small; its public consultation is self-selected. Neither establishes representative prevalence or willingness to pay. [European Commission SWD(2026) 996](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A52026SC0996)
- Federal Employment Agency shortage data provide hiring context, not evidence that an evidence-review tool shortens vacancies. [Skilled-trades report](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/en/press/2026-34-shortage-of-skilled-tradespeople-it-takes-242-days-to-fill-a-vacancy)

## Alternatives already available

- [Recognition in Germany](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/getting-recognition.php) provides official route guidance and responsible-authority discovery.
- [BQ-Portal](https://www.bq-portal.de/en/Companies-and-recognition) provides country-specific qualification-system information for companies and recognition bodies.
- [Recognition counselling / ZSBA](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de/html/en/information-about-counselling.php) provides free human guidance and application support.
- The [EU Skills Profile Tool](https://employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies-and-activities/skills-and-qualifications/skills-jobs/eu-skills-profile-tool-third-country-nationals_en) supports migrant skill profiling; the Commission says it is not a credential-authentication or formal-recognition tool.
- [TalentSure](https://talentsure.de/en/features/ai-matching) advertises international hiring, qualification/recognition tracking, a document hub, and compliance functions. It is a direct commercial alternative to investigate.

## Wedge to test

Do not recreate a public information chatbot, recognition finder, country database, or job matcher. Interview users about one specific case-preparation task: locating and reconciling applicant-provided evidence against a current official requirement list, then handing a reviewer a page-linked packet. The opportunity exists only if current official services, adviser routines, and commercial products leave repeated manual work that a buyer values enough to adopt or fund.

## Validation and stop gate

Interview 5–8 advisers / employer operations staff and five applicants who recently navigated a case. Ask for a walkthrough of the last case, tools used, rework, time, false alerts, authority requirements, and budget owner. Continue to a permissioned pilot only if at least three independent organizations report an unresolved recurring workflow and one agrees to evaluate a synthetic or permissioned pilot. Stop if existing tools meet the need or no accountable buyer emerges.

## Safety and regulatory context

The [EU AI Act consolidated text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727) lists certain recruitment and selection uses among high-risk use cases. A preparatory workflow may still be classified according to its intended purpose and deployment. The prototype excludes ranking, filtering, candidate evaluation, eligibility decisions, and hiring recommendations; this boundary is not a legal classification. Obtain qualified legal and data-protection advice before any real-data employer pilot.
