How SkillProof works

  1. Set your source.Choose a case and add requirements from a current official checklist.
  2. Inspect the original.Upload a permitted PDF or image. Extracted text and OCR are suggestions only.
  3. Link evidence.Capture a short exact excerpt with its source page. Correct and verify it yourself.
  4. Prepare a packet.Export a reviewer summary with requirement sources, page-linked evidence, and unresolved items.
What it does not decide

SkillProof does not authenticate a credential, determine equivalence or immigration eligibility, rank people, or recommend hiring decisions. Missing text is never a fraud finding.
